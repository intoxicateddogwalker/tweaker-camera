Your camera must let this page read its pictures. Some cameras block that. You still see the live picture, but the app can't check it. It will tell you.

Live view

LIVE

Watching live saves nothing. The app looks at the live picture, then throws it away. Only numbers are kept for people you name. New person? You see one picture. You name them. The picture is deleted right away. The only pictures kept are zone or strange events. They delete after 6 hours unless you save them. Everyone gets an alert. Only zone or strange events get a picture.

Draw a box on the live picture to watch one spot, like a gate. A person in the box gets an alert and one picture is saved. Outside the box, movement is only logged. But every person still gets an alert, in or out of the box. Drawing works with mouse or touch.

Bluetooth

Bluetooth can't do live video. It is too slow. This app does not offer it, and does not fake it. A Bluetooth-only camera can't be watched here.

Use a Wi-Fi camera or this phone's camera.

People the app remembers

Only numbers are kept. No photos. New person? You see one picture, name them, and it is deleted right away. This is not face recognition. Only people you named are ever named. Nobody is looked up. Strangers stay strangers. You are told about everyone, in or out of a zone. Only pictures are limited.

Cars the app has seen

Only numbers and times are kept. No photos. New car? You see one picture, say if it lives near here, and it is deleted right away. No plates are read. Nothing is looked up. A car seen 3 times in 7 days asks: does it live near here? You can change the number in Settings.

Saved pictures

One picture is saved only for a zone event or a strange event. It deletes after 6 hours unless you tap Save. Saved pictures stay. Nothing is uploaded. Alerts are different: you hear about every person, in or out of a zone.
